Why a spreadsheet instead of a guide

Our full moving budget breakdown and relocation budget calculator both answer the question once, for the numbers you type in that moment. A workbook stays useful after that: change your rent, add a second child, adjust your furnishing budget, and every total recalculates — and the Document Tracker gives you something to actually check off as the move happens, which a web page can't do.

Educational tool only — not financial or relocation advice. Government and utility fees change; confirm current figures before relying on this for a real move.
