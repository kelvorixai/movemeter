How this is calculated

This adds up the three cost phases described in our full moving budget breakdown, plus furnishing if your new place is unfurnished:

  • Flights + temporary housing: your own estimate — the most variable line item
  • Housing setup: security deposit (5% of annual rent) + agency commission (5% of annual rent) + Ejari registration (~AED 500) + DEWA connection deposit (~AED 2,000)
  • Personal admin: Emirates ID, medical testing, and related fees, estimated at ~AED 2,250 per relocating person
  • Furnishing: your own estimate, 0 if moving into a furnished place

This is a planning estimate, not a quote — actual costs vary by housing choice, family size, and employer relocation support. It doesn't include the rent cheques themselves, only the setup costs layered on top of them.

Frequently asked questions

What does a Dubai relocation budget cover beyond rent?

Housing setup costs (security deposit at 5% of annual rent, agency commission at 5%, Ejari registration ~AED 500, DEWA connection deposit ~AED 2,000), personal admin per person (~AED 2,250 for Emirates ID and medical testing), your flights and temporary housing budget, and furnishing if your new place isn't furnished.

How much is the security deposit when renting in Dubai?

Typically 5% of the annual rent, refundable at the end of the tenancy subject to any deductions for damage.

Does this calculator include the annual rent itself?

No — it only covers the one-time setup costs layered on top of the rent cheques: the deposit, agency fee, registration, personal admin, and furnishing.

What is Ejari and why does it cost money?

Ejari is Dubai's mandatory tenancy contract registration system. Registering it typically costs around AED 500 and is required before you can get DEWA (utilities) connected.
