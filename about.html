About MoveMeter

A small library of plain-English, data-driven guides on what relocating to the UAE actually costs — moving budgets, shipping methods, and the setup costs nobody plans for.

Most relocation content online is written by moving companies or relocation agencies trying to sell a service. MoveMeter deliberately isn't that. Every guide here breaks down the mechanics behind a cost figure — what a relocation phase actually involves and what it typically costs — using commonly cited ranges and general process knowledge, not a sales pitch.

Everything here is general education, not personalized relocation, financial, or logistics advice, and not tied to any moving company, freight forwarder, or government body. Figures are illustrative and based on commonly cited ranges at the time of writing — real costs vary by origin country, household size, and provider. For a number specific to your move, get quotes directly from licensed freight forwarders or relocation providers.
